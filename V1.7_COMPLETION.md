## Known limitations

Domain adapters remain intentionally conservative. Claim-level anticipation, literature relevance, market sizing/adoption, and organization-specific partner fit remain `WORK QUEUE` until their required schema fields and proposition-level support are present. These are evidence limitations, not pipeline failures.

## Version boundary

No new architecture is required for v1.7. Further improvements to domain-specific interpretation, source coverage, ranking, or specialized legal/market analysis belong in v1.8.
