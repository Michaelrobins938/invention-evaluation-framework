## Literature disclosures

1. **Syrjarinne (2000).** "Time recovery through fusion of inaccurate network timing
   assistance with GPS measurements." Proceedings of the Third International
   Conference on Information Fusion. DOI: 10.1109/IFIC.2000.859864. Pre-filing
   (2000) work on network timing assistance for GPS. Network-based assistance;
   does not disclose peer-mobile-as-server of GPS assistance data.
2. **Harper, Nicholson, Poon (2004).** "Process for improving GPS acquisition
   assistance data and server-side location determination for cellular networks."
   Journal of Global Positioning Systems 3(1):132. DOI: 10.5081/jgps.3.1.132.
   Post-filing (2004) server-side assistance; network-fixed-node architecture.
3. **Morosi, Del Re, Martinelli (2013).** "P2P cooperative GPS positioning with
   fine/coarse time assistance." 2013 ICL-GNSS. DOI: 10.1109/ICL-GNSS.2013.6577279.
   Post-filing (2013) peer-to-peer cooperative GPS positioning with time
   assistance — the closest academic analogue to the invention's peer-to-peer
   assistance concept, but published 12 years after the 2001 priority date.
4. **Morosi, Del Re, Martinelli (2014).** "Cooperative GPS positioning with
   peer-to-peer time assistance." 2014 VITAE. DOI: 10.1109/VITAE.2014.6934462.
   Post-filing follow-on to the 2013 work.
5. **Dovis, Chiasserini, Musumeci (2014).** "Context-aware peer-to-peer and
   cooperative positioning." 2014 ICL-GNSS. DOI: 10.1109/ICL-GNSS.2014.6934166.
   Post-filing context-aware P2P positioning.
6. **Chang, Li, Hou (2013).** "Peer-to-Peer Cooperative Positioning between GNSS
   Receivers." Applied Mechanics and Materials 341-342:614.
   DOI: 10.4028/www.scientific.net/AMM.341-342.614. Post-filing.
7. **Wirola, Halivaara (2007).** "On the Feasibility of Adding Carrier Phase
   Assistance to Cellular GNSS Assistance Standards." Journal of Global Positioning
   Systems 6(1):1. DOI: 10.5081/jgps.6.1.1. Post-filing; cellular GNSS assistance
   standards context (network-fixed-node).
8. **Sundaramurthy, Chayapathy, Kumar (2011).** "Wi-Fi assistance to SUPL-based
   Assisted-GPS simulators for indoor positioning." 2011 IEEE CCNC.
   DOI: 10.1109/CCNC.2011.5766641. Post-filing; SUPL (network-delivered A-GPS)
   context.

## Literature conclusion

- The **background objective** (GPS assistance data improves time-to-first-fix and
  sensitivity; delivered via cellular network) is well established in the
  literature, including pre-filing work (Syrjarinne 2000).
- The **peer-to-peer / cooperative GPS positioning** concept is actively researched
  in the literature, but the closest academic disclosures (Morosi 2013/2014, Dovis
  2014, Chang 2013) are all **post-filing** relative to the January 23, 2001
  priority date.
- **No pre-filing (pre-January 2001) academic publication was identified in the
  reviewed record that discloses the specific combination of a peer mobile terminal
  generating GPS assistance data from received satellite signals and transmitting
  that assistance data to a remote mobile terminal via the cellular network.**
  This is a negative search result (avenue metadata), not CONFIRMED ABSENT — the
  bounded universe is the Crossref queries run; IEEE Xplore and other databases
  were not independently searched in this run.
