## Design-space position

The invention sits in the "peer-aided GPS assistance" branch of the assisted-GPS
design space: it moves the assistance-data source from a fixed network node to a
peer mobile device, and adds hierarchical-group, trust, privacy, and scheduled-
sharing machinery. The dominant commercialization path for assisted GPS (network-
delivered assistance per 3GPP A-GPS/SUPL) remained the fixed-node architecture;
peer-to-peer assistance appears in the record as a recurring but secondary line
(see forward citations including Samsung, Broadcom, ITRI, and ad-hoc Bluetooth
assistance references). This is contextual evidence, not a patentability score.
