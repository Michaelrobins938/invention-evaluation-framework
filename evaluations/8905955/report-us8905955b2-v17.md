#### Short description

The patent describes a method for controlling a motorized exoskeleton using a control-panel locomotion selection, ground-force sensors, tilt sensing, joint-angle sensing, and safety logic. The system actuates motorized leg joints for standing, sitting, walking, and related locomotion modes, and halts or alerts when sensor feedback deviates from expected movement or indicates falling.

#### Detailed description

The disclosed exoskeleton includes trunk and leg braces, motorized joints, a controller, foot braces with ground-force sensors, a tilt sensor, a wireless wrist control unit, a power supply, motor-control boards, and programmed actions for walking, gait maintenance, sitting, standing, stair ascent, and stair descent. Ground-force patterns identify stances such as both legs loaded, leaning on one leg, foot touching, foot off, and falling. The controller selects actions based on the identified stance and can verify expected increasing or decreasing force, joint-angle changes, tilt, timeouts, user acknowledgments, and safety conditions.

#### Innovation claims from supplied material

- Use ground-force sensor patterns to identify user stance.
- Select and actuate locomotion actions based on the identified stance.
- Use leaning on one leg to initiate movement of the opposite or same leg.
- Use ground-force trends to verify standing and sitting procedures.
- Halt actuation when expected ground-force or joint-angle changes do not occur.
- Use tilt sensing to identify falling and halt or mitigate movement.
- Support walking, standing, sitting, stair ascent, and stair descent modes.
