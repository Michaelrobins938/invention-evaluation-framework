## 2. Partnership Strategy Recommendations

### For University of Utah / Blackrock Neurotech

**Recommended approach**: Maintain current exclusive licensing relationship; expand IP portfolio around next-generation flexible arrays (Neuralace).

**Key actions**:
1. Continue development of Neuralace for 10,000+ channel arrays
2. Pursue FDA approval for clinical BCI (MoveAgain device)
3. Expand into visual prosthesis market (first-in-human ~2028)
4. License peripheral nerve applications to specialized companies

### For Potential Licensees

**Recommended approach**: Target specific applications where Utah Array has demonstrated value but market is underserved.

**Opportunity areas**:
1. **Visual prosthesis**: High unmet need; limited competition
2. **Peripheral nerve interfaces**: Utah Slanted Electrode Array applications
3. **Epilepsy monitoring**: Chronic intracortical recording
4. **Research tools**: Custom arrays for specific research applications

## 3. Due Diligence Items

For any potential partnership or licensing:

1. **IP status verification**: Confirm patent family status; expired patents vs. active continuations
2. **Licensing terms**: Review existing Blackrock/University of Utah agreement
3. **Manufacturing capability**: Utah Array requires specialized MEMS fabrication
4. **Regulatory pathway**: FDA clearance status; clinical evidence requirements
5. **Clinical relationships**: Access to BCI research centers and clinical trial sites
6. **Competitive position**: Assess freedom to operate vs. improvement patents
