## 2. Short description
A nano-scaled graphene plate material comprising at least one dimension ≤100 nm and a process for producing it by partial/full carbonization/graphitization of a precursor polymer or pitch to form polymeric carbon with graphite crystallites, exfoliating those crystallites, and mechanical attrition to nanometer scale.

## 3. Detailed description
The invention relates to nano-scaled thin-plate carbon material, nano-scaled graphene plate (NGP), and a process for producing it.
Step a) partially or fully carbonize a precursor polymer or heat-treat petroleum/coal tar pitch to produce polymeric carbon containing micron- and/or nanometer-scaled graphite crystallites, each crystallite comprising one or multiple sheets of graphite plane.
Step b) exfoliate graphite crystallites in polymeric carbon by chemical treatment, intercalation, foaming/heating.
Step c) subject exfoliated polymeric carbon to mechanical attrition treatment to produce nano-scaled graphene plate material.
Preferred embodiments: heat treatment 300-1,000°C for partial carbonization, 1,000-3,000°C for graphitization; exfoliation with oxidizing acids, intercalants, blowing agents; attrition by ball milling. NGPs can be single-layer or stacks <20 layers, length/width/thickness <100 nm, preferably <10 nm.
Applications: reinforcement in polymer, ceramic, metal, carbon, glass matrices; improved mechanical, electrical, thermal properties at low loadings ~1-5 vol%.

## 4. Background / related research
Carbon nanotubes are known but expensive due to low yield/purification. Alternatives sought. Prior art includes epitaxial carbon film by sublimation decomposition of SiC, CVD synthesis, arc discharge. No prior scalable low-cost NGP process.

## 5. Innovation claims stated in the patent
- Claim 1: nano-scaled graphene plate material comprising at least a nanometer-scaled plate with sheet(s) of graphite plane, two-dimensional hexagonal lattice of carbon atoms, length/width parallel to plane and thickness orthogonal, at least one dimension ≤100 nm.
- Claim 2: composite material containing matrix + predetermined proportion of nano-scaled graphene plate material as reinforcement.
- Claim 3: composite with ≤15 vol% NGP.
- Claim 4: composite with ≤5 vol% NGP.
- Claim 5: matrix selected from polymer, metal, ceramic, carbon, glass, organic, or combination.
Process claims: carbonization/exfoliation/mechanical attrition sequence with specific temperature ranges, intercalation/oxidation, expansion, ball milling.

## 6. Proof-of-concept status
Examples 1-6 describe preparation from PAN fibers, phenol formaldehyde resin, coal tar pitch, petroleum pitch, with oxidation, intercalation, exfoliation, ball milling producing nanometer-sized powder. Composite examples with PMMA, epoxy show increased tensile failure strain and mechanical improvement.
Proof-of-concept status: described in patent examples; independent verification not established from record alone.
