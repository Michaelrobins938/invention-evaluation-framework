- High-speed rail and maglev infrastructure.
- Electrodynamic suspension subsystems.
- Superconducting magnet systems.
- Conductive guideway and track-loop infrastructure.
- Cryogenic support and maintenance systems.
- Future evacuated-tube or low-pressure transport concepts.

## Commercial barriers

- Guideway construction and maintenance cost.
- Superconducting magnet cryogenics and quench protection.
- Transitional-speed and propulsion requirements.
- Safety systems for loss of superconductivity or power.
- Track-loop conductor losses and thermal management.
- Alignment tolerances and route infrastructure.
- Regulatory approval for high-speed passenger transport.
- Historical uncertainty about actual prototype, pilot, or production deployment.
