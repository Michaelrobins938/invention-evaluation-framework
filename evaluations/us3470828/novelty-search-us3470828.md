# IP and Novelty Analysis — US 3,470,828

## Analytical claim

The target claim representation is a provisional evaluation claim derived from supplied claim 1. It is not a legal claim construction.

Claim 1 requires, in substance:

1. A ground vehicle.
2. A plurality of superconducting magnets carried by the vehicle.
3. A track bed formed by a longitudinally extending series of oriented shorted loops.
4. The loops are composed of metal conductors.
5. The magnets and loops are in electromagnetic inductive relationship.
6. Above a transitional speed, the relationship generates suspension force.
7. The force floats the vehicle and maintains it at an equilibrium position above the track bed.
8. The vehicle is stabilized against vertical displacement.

```text
Known magnetic levitation or suspension

  → passive longitudinal shorted-loop track arrays
  → induced lift with speed threshold
  → vertical equilibrium stabilization
```
