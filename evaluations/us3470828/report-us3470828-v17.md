# Invention Evaluation Report — US 3,470,828

> Preliminary evidence-constrained evaluation based on the supplied patent PDF and selected public patent records. This is not legal advice, not a formal patentability opinion, and not an FTO opinion.

## Executive Summary

US 3,470,828 discloses a technically detailed electrodynamic suspension and stabilization system for a high-speed ground vehicle. The core architecture places superconducting magnets on the moving vehicle and uses longitudinal arrays of shorted, non-magnetic conductive loops in the track bed. The patent describes induced-current lift, lateral restoring forces, vertical stabilization, oscillation damping, alternative loop geometries, and current-limiting inductor/diode arrangements.

The primary patent record establishes the patent identity, inventors, filing date, publication date, claims, classifications, and technical disclosure. Related pre-filing records were also retrieved: US1020943A discloses a levitating carrier using magnets and a non-magnetic conductive guideway; US3125964A discloses magnetic vehicle suspension and gap control. Neither retrieved reference was mapped as disclosing every limitation of the provisional claim-1 representation. Anticipation therefore remains **UNRESOLVED — SEARCH-INCOMPLETE**, not confirmed absent.

The technical disclosure is strong at the patent-record level, but independent experimental validation, complete historical literature recovery, family/legal-status verification, quantitative market evidence, deployment history, and organization-specific partner fit remain unresolved. Commercial actionability is **Indeterminate-to-Limited** on the completed evidence.

## 1. Technology Analysis

### 1.1 Technology profile

See `technology-profile-us3470828.md`. The system is a passive electrodynamic suspension architecture combining superconducting vehicle magnets, conductive track-loop arrays, geometry-dependent restoring forces, long damping loops, and current-limiting inductors.

### 1.2 Technical evidence

The primary record directly supports the existence of the disclosed architecture and claims. The patent's theoretical power, train-size, speed, lift, and force figures are recorded as patent disclosure claims. They are not independently validated performance findings.

## 2. Patent Landscape Analysis

### 2.1 Landscape result

See `patent-landscape-us3470828.md`. Earlier magnetic levitation and suspension records were located, but a complete family-deduplicated quantitative landscape was not established.

### 2.2 Design-space position

The target patent explores a broad design space: vertical lift loops, figure-eight lateral loops, long damping loops, buried or road-level loops, alternate orientations, and grouped current-limiting circuits. This is evidence of a broad architecture exploration event, not by itself evidence of patentability strength.

## 3. IP / Novelty Analysis

### 3.1 Provisional evaluation claim

The claim representation is analytical and not a legal claim construction. It requires the vehicle, superconducting magnets, longitudinal shorted conductive loops, inductive relationship, speed-dependent lift, equilibrium position, and vertical stabilization.

### 3.2 Prior-art mapping

US1020943A and US3125964A disclose related magnetic vehicle concepts but differ in important ways. The retrieved evidence does not establish that either one alone discloses the complete superconducting passive shorted-loop architecture and vertical restoring relationship.

### 3.3 Anticipation gate

No anticipation finding is admitted. Claim-level search and mapping remain incomplete.

### 3.4 Obviousness and causal bridge

The bridge from earlier magnetic levitation to the target combination requires evidence for the specific use of superconducting vehicle magnets, passive longitudinal shorted-loop arrays, speed-dependent induced lift, and vertical equilibrium stabilization. Motivation, compatibility, reasonable expectation of success, and unexpected technical result are not established.

### 3.5 Chronology

The target filing/priority date is recorded as 1967-11-21 from the primary patent record. Earlier retrieved patent references include US1020943A (1911 priority) and US3125964A (1964 publication). A complete family and prosecution chronology remains in recovery.

## 4. Literature Analysis

The target patent names Powell's 1963 ASME paper, Van Beelen et al.'s 1965 *Physica* paper, and Powell/Danby's 1966 ASME report. Their citation identity is present in the patent, but full independent retrieval and proposition-level mapping were not completed. No independent literature disclosure is admitted as a final finding.

## 5. Market Analysis

The plausible market boundary includes maglev/high-speed rail infrastructure, superconducting magnet systems, conductive guideways, cryogenic support, and future low-pressure transport. No reconstructable market size, CAGR, shipment, revenue, adoption, or current product-identity finding was established.

Commercial barriers include guideway cost, cryogenic operation, quench and safety management, alignment, transitional-speed operation, infrastructure approvals, and reliability requirements.

## 6. Opportunity Assessment

The technology is technically distinctive at the patent-disclosure level but commercially constrained by infrastructure intensity and unresolved deployment evidence. Modern resurrection could be investigated using contemporary superconductors, sensing, control, power electronics, and materials, but that is an analytical opportunity hypothesis rather than an established market conclusion.
