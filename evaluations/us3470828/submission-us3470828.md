## Short description

The patent describes a high-speed ground vehicle suspension and stabilization system using superconducting magnets carried by the vehicle and longitudinal arrays of shorted, non-magnetic conductive loops in the track bed. Electromagnetic induction generates lift, lateral and vertical restoring forces, and damping of oscillatory motion while the vehicle is moving above a transitional speed.

## Detailed description

The disclosed system places superconducting loops on a moving train and shorted conductor loops in or around a track bed. The moving superconducting magnets create changing magnetic flux over the track loops, inducing currents in the track conductors. Interaction between the train magnetic fields and induced track currents generates vertical lift and can stabilize the vehicle against vertical, lateral, rotational, and oscillatory displacement.

The disclosure describes vertical lifting loop arrays, figure-eight lateral-stability loops, long horizontal and vertical damping loops, alternative loop geometries, below-roadbed arrangements, and diode-connected external inductors for limiting induced current. The vehicle may use wheels at rest and at transitional speeds, with magnetic suspension becoming effective above a stated transition speed. The patent discusses a nominal 100-foot, 60,000-pound passenger train, superconducting niobium-titanium loops cooled near 4 K, liquid-helium cooling, insulation, shielding, and estimated suspension power.

## Inventor-stated objectives and technical elements

- Provide electromagnetic suspension for a high-speed ground vehicle.
- Use superconducting vehicle magnets and ordinary conductive track loops.
- Generate vertical lift without a continuously superconductive track.
- Stabilize the vehicle laterally and vertically around an equilibrium position.
- Dampen horizontal and vertical oscillations.
- Permit wheel support at rest and during transitional speeds.
- Limit induced track-loop current using external inductors and unidirectional devices.
- Permit alternative track-loop arrangements, including arrangements level with or beneath the roadbed.
