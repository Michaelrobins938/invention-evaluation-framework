# Literature Search — US 8,527,057 B2

## Search scope

Queries covered retinal prosthesis, epiretinal implant, retinal electrode array,
inductive retinal implant, hermetic implant package, and retinal-tack fixation.
Primary literature source: PubMed. Adjacent technical/regulatory sources: NEI and FDA.

## Literature disclosures

1. Margalit E, Maia M, Weiland JD, et al. “Retinal prosthesis for the blind.” *Survey
   of Ophthalmology*. 2002;47(4):335–356. DOI: 10.1016/S0039-6257(02)00311-9.
   PubMed PMID 12161210. Review of neuronal electrical stimulation and retinal
   prosthesis architectures. Supports the background objective and epiretinal context;
   does not establish the target package geometry.
2. Luo YH, da Cruz L. “The Argus II Retinal Prosthesis System.” *Progress in Retinal
   and Eye Research*. 2016;50:89–107. DOI: 10.1016/j.preteyeres.2015.09.003.
   PubMed PMID 26404104. Reviews the Argus II system and its clinical/regulatory
   significance. Supports the existence of a commercial retinal-prosthesis pathway;
   does not establish embodiment of every claimed package limitation.
3. Goetz GA, Palanker DV. “Electronic approaches to restoration of sight.” *Reports
   on Progress in Physics*. 2016;79(9):096701. DOI: 10.1088/0034-4885/79/9/096701.
   PubMed PMID 27502748. Reviews epiretinal and subretinal approaches. Supports field
   orientation and technology alternatives.
4. Ghani N, Bansal J, Naidu A, Chaudhary KM. “Long term positional stability of the
   Argus II retinal prosthesis epiretinal implant.” *BMC Ophthalmology*. 2023;23:70.
   DOI: 10.1186/s12886-022-02736-w. PubMed PMID 36797684. Addresses long-term
   implant positional stability and is relevant to fixation/implantation risk; it does
   not prove the specific US8527057 package was used.
5. Palanker D. “Electronic Retinal Prostheses.” *Cold Spring Harbor Perspectives in
   Medicine*. 2023;13(8):a041525. DOI: 10.1101/cshperspect.a041525. PubMed PMID
   36781222. Review of retinal prosthesis architectures and stimulation targets;
   useful background, not a claim-level disclosure of the target package.

## Literature conclusion

The literature confirms the technical objective, retinal stimulation context, and
clinical importance of fixation and implant stability. It does not, from the reviewed
records, establish the complete strap + coplanar coil + hermetic flip-chip package
combination. A broader engineering-database search for hermetic implant packaging and
manufacturing disclosures remains a work item.
