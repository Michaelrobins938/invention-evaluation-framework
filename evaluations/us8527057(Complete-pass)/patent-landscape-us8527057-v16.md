## Design-space position

The invention is a convergence point between three technical streams: (1) retinal
electrode fixation and flexible arrays, (2) inductive power/data coupling, and (3)
hermetic miniaturized implant electronics. The same-assignee references indicate
progressive exploration of individual modules before integration into the strap-mounted
low-profile ocular configuration. This is contextual evidence, not a patentability score.
