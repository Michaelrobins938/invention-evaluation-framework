## 3. Short description

The patent describes an implantable retinal prosthesis in which a retinal electrode
array, hermetic electronics package, and secondary inductive receiving coil are
mechanically arranged on a strap surrounding the sclera. The electronics package
uses a ceramic substrate with conductive vias, a flip-chip ASIC, wire-bonded
passive components, and a laser-welded metal lid; the package and coil are arranged
to reduce the height of the implant above the sclera.

## 4. Detailed description

The preferred implant includes an epiretinal flexible electrode array connected by
a cable through the sclera to an electronics package. A secondary inductive coil
receives power and data from an external primary coil. A molded silicone body holds
the package and coil in an end-to-end or alternative surrounding arrangement and
narrows into a strap with suture tabs and, in an embodiment, a surgical-tool hook.

The hermetic package includes an electrically non-conductive ceramic substrate,
metallized conductive vias, thin-film metallization, a flip-chip bonded ASIC, a
hybrid substrate carrying passive components, gold wire bonds, a brazed metal case
wall, and a laser-welded metal lid. The specification describes vacuum bake-out,
getter material, biocompatible metals, underfill, silicone overmolding, and a
low-profile geometry intended to reduce conjunctival irritation and obstruction of
eye movement.

## 6. Innovation claims stated in the patent

- **Claim 1 core combination:** an electrode array; a sclera-surrounding strap; a
  hermetic electronics package containing a flip-chip circuit mounted to the strap;
  an electrical cable coupling the array and package; and a secondary inductive
  coil mounted to the strap, coplanar with the package, electrically coupled to it,
  powering the flip-chip circuit, and suitable for lateral scleral placement.
- Dependent features include suture tabs, a fan tail, a surgical-tool hook, a
  polymer-sandwiched metal-trace cable, cable folding, a wound-wire coil, and an
  oval-shaped coil.
- **Manufacturing disclosure:** ceramic substrate with conductive vias, flip-chip
  bonding, wire-bonded passive stack, brazed case wall, vacuum bake-out, optional
  getter, and laser-welded metal lid forming a hermetic package.
- The patent's stated design objective is a compact, low-profile implant that
  integrates the coil and electronics package while maintaining hermeticity and
  retinal stimulation functionality.
