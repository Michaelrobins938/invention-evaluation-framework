# Technology Profile — US 8,527,057 B2

## Plain-language description

This is an implantable epiretinal prosthesis that combines a retinal electrode
array with a hermetic ASIC package and an inductive receiving coil mounted on a
scleral strap. The package architecture uses ceramic vias, flip-chip and wire-bond
interconnects, a brazed case wall, vacuum bake-out, and a laser-welded lid to create
a compact implant intended to reduce tissue irritation and obstruction of eye motion.

## Innovation assessment

- The claimed delta is not electrical retinal stimulation by itself; it is the
  integrated low-profile mechanical arrangement and hermetic package architecture.
- The invention combines known elements from retinal electrodes, inductive coupling,
  ceramic feedthroughs, flip-chip packaging, and implant fixation. This creates
  **combination-obviousness exposure**; the relevant question is whether the specific
  ocular packaging arrangement and manufacturing sequence were directly motivated.
- Specific technical elements claimed as unique include the strap-mounted package
  and coil, coplanar/low-profile arrangement, and the particular hermetic package
  construction with mixed flip-chip and wire-bonded circuitry.
- Same-assignee sibling filings show a design-space exploration around retinal
  electrode arrays, side-mounted coils, implant packages, bonding, feedthroughs,
  and flexible circuits. This patent occupies the low-profile ocular integration /
  hermetic-package region of that design space.

## Regulatory scope estimate

The technology is an implantable medical device. FDA's Overview of Device Regulation
identifies device classification, establishment registration/listing, premarket
pathways, IDE requirements for clinical studies, QMSR/21 CFR Part 820, labeling, and
medical-device reporting as applicable regulatory controls. Scope-level burden is
**High**, but product classification and pathway selection require device-specific
regulatory analysis.

## Development stage

described manufacturing sequence, but prototype, validation, clinical, and production
evidence are not established from this intake. Next milestones: hermeticity testing;
fatigue and ocular-motion testing; biocompatibility; electrical stimulation bench tests;
animal/clinical protocol; and regulatory pathway confirmation.
