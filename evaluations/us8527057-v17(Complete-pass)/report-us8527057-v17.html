3. Short description

The patent describes an implantable retinal prosthesis in which a retinal electrode array, hermetic electronics package, and secondary inductive receiving coil are mechanically arranged on a strap surrounding the sclera. The electronics package uses a ceramic substrate with conductive vias, a flip-chip ASIC, wire-bonded passive components, and a laser-welded metal lid; the package and coil are arranged to reduce the height of the implant above the sclera.

4. Detailed description

The preferred implant includes an epiretinal flexible electrode array connected by a cable through the sclera to an electronics package. A secondary inductive coil receives power and data from an external primary coil. A molded silicone body holds the package and coil in an end-to-end or alternative surrounding arrangement and narrows into a strap with suture tabs and, in an embodiment, a surgical-tool hook.

The hermetic package includes an electrically non-conductive ceramic substrate, metallized conductive vias, thin-film metallization, a flip-chip bonded ASIC, a hybrid substrate carrying passive components, gold wire bonds, a brazed metal case wall, and a laser-welded metal lid. The specification describes vacuum bake-out, getter material, biocompatible metals, underfill, silicone overmolding, and a low-profile geometry intended to reduce conjunctival irritation and obstruction of eye movement.

5. Background / related research identified in the record

The patent discusses electrical stimulation of retinal and neural tissue, early phosphene experiments, retinal tacks, epiretinal electrode arrays, implantable hermetic packages, and low-profile implant enclosures. It cites or discusses de Juan 5,109,844; Humayun 5,935,155; Berrang US 2003/0109903; and multiple related Second Sight / Greenberg applications concerning hermetic feedthroughs, biocompatible bonding, side-mounted inductive coils, implantable-device packages, and flexible-circuit electrode arrays.

  • The patent's stated design objective is a compact, low-profile implant that integrates the coil and electronics package while maintaining hermeticity and retinal stimulation functionality.
