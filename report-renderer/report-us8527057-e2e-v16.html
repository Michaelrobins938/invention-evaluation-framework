Technology Analysis

2.1 Technology profile

The invention is a low-profile ocular implant. An epiretinal electrode array connects through a scleral cable to an electronics package, while a secondary inductive coil receives power and data from an external coil. A molded silicone body and strap hold the elements around the sclera. The electronics package combines a ceramic substrate, conductive vias, thin-film metallization, a flip-chip ASIC, a wire-bonded passive stack, a brazed case wall, and a laser-welded lid.

Invention: Retinal Prosthesis and Method of Manufacturing a Retinal Prosthesis. Inventors: Robert J. Greenberg; Jerry Ok; Jordan Neysmith; Kevin Wilkins; Neil Hamilton Talbot; Da-Yu Chang. Original assignee: Second Sight Medical Products, Inc., Sylmar, California. Priority: US 60/675,980, filed 2005-04-28. Parent: US 11/385,315, filed 2006-03-20; issued as US 7,881,799. Divisional: US 12/976,650, filed 2010-12-22. Publication: US 2011/0118807 A1, published 2011-05-19. Grant: US 8,527,057 B2, granted 2013-09-03. Classification shown on patent: A61N 1/18; USPC 607/54.

Short description: An implantable epiretinal prosthesis combines a retinal electrode array, a hermetic electronics package, and a secondary inductive receiving coil on a sclera-surrounding strap. The package uses ceramic vias, flip-chip ASIC attachment, wire-bonded passive components, a brazed case wall, and a laser-welded lid.

Detailed description: A flexible epiretinal array connects through a scleral cable to the package. The secondary coil receives power and data from an external primary coil. A silicone body holds the package and coil in an end-to-end or surrounding arrangement and narrows into a strap with suture tabs and an optional surgical-tool hook. The package process includes vacuum bake-out, getter material, biocompatible metals, underfill, silicone overmolding, brazing, and laser welding.

Innovation claims: Claim 1 recites the electrode array, scleral strap, hermetic flip-chip package, coupling cable, and coplanar strap-mounted secondary coil. Dependent features include suture tabs, fan tail, hook, polymer-sandwiched metal-trace cable, cable folding, wound-wire coil, and oval coil.
